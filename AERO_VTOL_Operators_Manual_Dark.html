SECTION 03

Step-by-Step Standard Operating Procedure (5-Step Wizard)

STEP 01 · Payload & Mission Requirements

Enter Internal Bay Payload (kg\text{kg}kg), External Drop Payload (kg\text{kg}kg), and External Pod Dimensions L×W×HL \times W \times HL×W×H (mm\text{mm}mm). The solver automatically adjusts the fuselage length and widens the spanwise VTOL boom spacing so propellers clear the external pod. Set Target Cruise Speed (km/h\text{km/h}km/h), Design Altitude ASL (m\text{m}m), and Ambient Temp (∘C^\circ\text{C}∘C) to establish the ISA atmospheric density ρ\rhoρ and cold-weather battery derating.

STEP 02 · Wing, Airfoil & Carbon Spar Sizing

Select from five proven low-Reynolds airfoils (SG6043, SD7062, NACA 2412, Clark-Y, or Eppler E423). Tune Target Wing Loading (g/dm2\text{g/dm}^2g/dm2), Aspect Ratio (AR\text{AR}AR), Taper Ratio (λ\lambdaλ), Tip Washout (∘^\circ∘), and Ultimate Pull-Up Load Factor (GGG). Verify in the live structural card that the selected 3K carbon main spar (∅18–24 mm\varnothing 18–24\text{ mm}∅18–24 mm) maintains a Safety Factor SF>2.0×\text{SF} > 2.0\timesSF>2.0×.

STEP 03 · VTOL Propulsion & Propeller Suite

Choose QuadPlane (4 Lift Motors) or Coaxial Octo-QuadPlane (8 Motors), Hover Thrust-to-Weight (T/W\text{T/W}T/W), and Battery Chemistry (`6S2P Li-Ion`, `8S2P Li-Ion`, or `6S LiPo`). Select between Off-the-Shelf Commercial Propellers (with 3 market alternatives per rotor) and Custom BEMT 3D-Printed Propellers (click Propeller Alternatives & Custom CAD → to export `.STL` and `.STEP` files for CW/CCW hover and pusher propellers).

STEP 04 · FAA CG vs. Center of Lift (CL), Sensor Placement & PX4

Use the Battery Fore/Aft Trim Offset Slider (±80 mm\pm 80\text{ mm}±80 mm) and toggle Ext. Payload Attached while inspecting the live side-profile balance diagram. Verify that CG\text{CG}CG stays ahead of the Neutral Point (NP=39% MAC\text{NP} = 39\%\text{ MAC}NP=39% MAC) with a Static Margin of 8%–15% MAC8\%–15\%\text{ MAC}8%–15% MAC both before and after cargo drop. Select your MicoAir MTF-02P / MTF-02 optical flow sensor and inspect the EMI-isolated mounting coordinates (X mmX\text{ mm}X mm from the nose tip).

STEP 05 · Manufacturing, PX4 SITL & CFD Export Hub

Download the complete Anycubic Kobra S1 Max `.ZIP` bundle (all segmented `.STL` plates, `.X_T`, `.STEP`, `.DAT` airfoil curves, custom BEMT propellers, `quadplane_vtol.params`, and `spec.json`), export CFD/stability bundles, download the `.CSV` Bill of Materials, or push directly to GitHub.
