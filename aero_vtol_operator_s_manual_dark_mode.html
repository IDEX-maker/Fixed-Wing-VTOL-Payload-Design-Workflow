SECTION 01

System Overview & Complete Capability Matrix

AERO-VTOL QuadPlane Studio is a self-contained parametric aerospace design, structural analysis, additive manufacturing segmentation, and autopilot configuration suite. Every input adjustment triggers a real-time recalculation across 15 integrated engineering modules:

# Engineering Module Key Outputs & Functional Capabilities
01ISA Altitude & Cold-Weather DeratingComputes air density $\rho$ from ISA barometric laws ($0–4,500\text{ m}$ ASL, $-35^\circ\text{C}\text{ to }+45^\circ\text{C}$) and applies automatic sub-zero battery capacity derating up to $30\%$.
025-Airfoil High-Lift Aerodynamic SolverSupports Selig SG6043, SD7062, NACA 2412, Clark-Y, and Eppler E423. Solves wingspan $b$, root/tip chord, $\text{MAC}$, stall speed $V_{\text{stall}}$, Reynolds number $\text{Re}$, Mach, and $L/D$.
03Carbon Spar Bending & Shear SizingCalculates root bending moment $M_{\text{root}}$ at ultimate load factor $n_{\text{ult}}$ ($2.0\text{G}–6.0\text{G}$), selects 3K roll-wrapped carbon tube diameter ($\varnothing 18–24\text{ mm}$), and verifies Safety Factor ($\text{SF}$).
04Control Surface & Servo Torque SizingSizes aileron ($22\%\text{ MAC}$) and elevator ($28\%\text{ tail chord}$) chords and computes minimum metal-gear servo stall torque ($\text{kg}\cdot\text{cm}$).
05Motor, ESC & Battery RecommenderMatches commercial brushless motors (T-Motor MN4014 / MN5008 / AT3520 / AT4120, Hobbywing XRotor, SunnySky, Dualsky), BLHeli_32 ESCs, and Molicel P45B Li-Ion or LiPo packs.
06OTS Market + Custom BEMT PropellersRecommends primary + 3 backup commercial propellers OR generates custom 2/3-blade Blade Element Momentum Theory (BEMT) 3D-printed propellers in watertight `.STL` and `.STEP`.
07Transition Energy & Distance CorridorComputes transition speed ($1.25\,V_{\text{stall}}$), acceleration time, forward transition distance, energy ($\text{Wh}$), and back-transition braking flare distance.
08Propeller-to-Wing & Pod ClearancePositions twin carbon booms spanwise to clear the external payload pod and extends boom length so rotor tips clear wing leading/trailing edges by $>25\text{ mm}$.

10Avionics & MicoAir MTF-02/02P PlacementComputes exact longitudinal mounting stations ($X\text{ mm}$ from nose datum) for Battery, PX4 FC, ESCs, GNSS mast, RC receiver, Pitot, and MicoAir MTF-02 / MTF-02P Optical Flow + ToF rangefinder.

SECTION 03

Step-by-Step Standard Operating Procedure (5-Step Wizard)

STEP 01 · Payload & Mission Requirements

Enter Internal Bay Payload ($\text{kg}$), External Drop Payload ($\text{kg}$), and External Pod Dimensions $L \times W \times H$ ($\text{mm}$). The solver automatically adjusts the fuselage length and widens the spanwise VTOL boom spacing so propellers clear the external pod. Set Target Cruise Speed ($\text{km/h}$), Design Altitude ASL ($\text{m}$), and Ambient Temp ($^\circ\text{C}$) to establish the ISA atmospheric density $\rho$ and cold-weather battery derating.

STEP 02 · Wing, Airfoil & Carbon Spar Sizing

Select from five proven low-Reynolds airfoils (SG6043, SD7062, NACA 2412, Clark-Y, or Eppler E423). Tune Target Wing Loading ($\text{g/dm}^2$), Aspect Ratio ($\text{AR}$), Taper Ratio ($\lambda$), Tip Washout ($^\circ$), and Ultimate Pull-Up Load Factor ($G$). Verify in the live structural card that the selected 3K carbon main spar ($\varnothing 18–24\text{ mm}$) maintains a Safety Factor $\text{SF} > 2.0\times$.

STEP 03 · VTOL Propulsion & Propeller Suite

Choose QuadPlane (4 Lift Motors) or Coaxial Octo-QuadPlane (8 Motors), Hover Thrust-to-Weight ($\text{T/W}$), and Battery Chemistry (`6S2P Li-Ion`, `8S2P Li-Ion`, or `6S LiPo`). Select between Off-the-Shelf Commercial Propellers (with 3 market alternatives per rotor) and Custom BEMT 3D-Printed Propellers (click Propeller Alternatives & Custom CAD → to export `.STL` and `.STEP` files for CW/CCW hover and pusher propellers).

STEP 04 · FAA CG vs. Center of Lift (CL), Sensor Placement & PX4

Use the Battery Fore/Aft Trim Offset Slider ($\pm 80\text{ mm}$) and toggle Ext. Payload Attached while inspecting the live side-profile balance diagram. Verify that $\text{CG}$ stays ahead of the Neutral Point ($\text{NP} = 39\%\text{ MAC}$) with a Static Margin of $8\%–15\%\text{ MAC}$ both before and after cargo drop. Select your MicoAir MTF-02P / MTF-02 optical flow sensor and inspect the EMI-isolated mounting coordinates ($X\text{ mm}$ from the nose tip).

STEP 05 · Manufacturing, PX4 SITL & CFD Export Hub

Download the complete Anycubic Kobra S1 Max `.ZIP` bundle (all segmented `.STL` plates, `.X_T`, `.STEP`, `.DAT` airfoil curves, custom BEMT propellers, `quadplane_vtol.params`, and `spec.json`), export CFD/stability bundles, download the `.CSV` Bill of Materials, or push directly to GitHub.

SECTION 04

Deep-Dive Engineering Modules & Mathematical Formulations

4.1 ISA Atmospheric Density & Cold-Weather Battery Derating

At design altitude $h$ ($\text{m}$ ASL) and ambient temperature $T_{\text{amb}}$ ($^\circ\text{C}$), air density $\rho$ and sub-zero usable battery energy $E_{\text{usable}}$ are solved as:

$$T_K = T_{\text{amb}} + 273.15, \qquad P = 101325 \left(1 - \frac{0.0065 \, h}{288.15}\right)^{5.255} \text{ [Pa]}, \qquad \rho = \frac{P}{287.05 \, T_K} \text{ [kg/m}^3\text{]}$$ $$\eta_{\text{temp}} = \min\left(0.30,\; 0.009 \, |T_{\text{amb}}|\right) \text{ for } T_{\text{amb}} < 0^\circ\text{C}, \qquad E_{\text{usable}} = 0.85 \, E_{\text{nominal}} \left(1 - \eta_{\text{temp}}\right)$$

4.2 Wing Planform, Stall Speed, Transition Corridor & Cruise L/D

Given Maximum Takeoff Weight $\text{MTOW}$ ($\text{kg}$), wing loading $W/S$ ($\text{g/dm}^2$), aspect ratio $\text{AR}$, and taper ratio $\lambda = c_t / c_r$:

$$S = \frac{\text{MTOW} \times 10}{W/S} \text{ [m}^2\text{]}, \qquad b = \sqrt{S \cdot \text{AR}} \text{ [m]}, \qquad c_r = \frac{2S}{b(1 + \lambda)}, \qquad \text{MAC} = \frac{2}{3} c_r \left(\frac{1 + \lambda + \lambda^2}{1 + \lambda}\right)$$ $$V_{\text{stall}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \, \text{MTOW} \, g}{\rho \, S \, C_{L,\max}}}, \qquad V_{\text{trans}} = 1.25 \, V_{\text{stall}}, \qquad \frac{L}{D} = \frac{C_{L,\text{cruise}}}{C_{D0} + \frac{C_{L,\text{cruise}}^2}{\pi \, \text{AR} \, e} + 0.008}$$

4.3 Carbon Main Spar Root Bending Stress & Safety Factor

Under ultimate pull-up load factor $n_{\text{ult}}$, root bending moment $M_{\text{root}}$, tube moment of inertia $I$, peak stress $\sigma_{\max}$, and Safety Factor ($\text{SF}$ against $\sigma_{\text{ult}} = 650\text{ MPa}$) are:

$$M_{\text{root}} = \left(\frac{\text{MTOW} \cdot g \cdot n_{\text{ult}}}{2}\right) \left(0.42 \, \frac{b}{2}\right), \qquad I = \frac{\pi}{64}\left(D_o^4 - D_i^4\right), \qquad \sigma_{\max} = \frac{M_{\text{root}}(D_o/2)}{I}, \qquad \text{SF} = \frac{650\text{ MPa}}{\sigma_{\max}/10^6}$$

4.4 FAA Longitudinal Stability (CG, Aerodynamic Center & Neutral Point) & BEMT Blade Twist

$$X_{\text{AC}} = X_{\text{LE}} + 0.25\,\text{MAC}, \qquad X_{\text{NP}} = X_{\text{LE}} + 0.39\,\text{MAC}, \qquad \text{SM} = \frac{X_{\text{NP}} - X_{\text{CG}}}{\text{MAC}} \times 100\%$$ $$\beta(r) = \arctan\left(\frac{P_{\text{geom}}}{2 \pi r}\right), \qquad c(r) = 0.22 R \sin\left(\pi \left(\frac{r}{R}\right)^{0.75}\right) + 7\text{ mm}$$

SECTION 07

PX4 SITL Simulation & Controller Setup Guide

Gazebo Harmonic SITL Launch
export PX4_HOME_LAT=53.5461
export PX4_HOME_LON=-113.4938
export PX4_HOME_ALT=670
make px4_sitl gz_standard_vtol

In QGroundControl, navigate to Vehicle Setup → Parameters → Tools → Load from file and load `quadplane_vtol.params` (`SYS_AUTOSTART 13000` + MicoAir MTF-02P UART parameters).

Supported QGroundControl Controllers
  • Xbox Series X|S Game Controller: Enable Joystick, select Mode 2, check "Throttle sticks to center"; map Y = VTOL Transition, A = Arm, B = RTL.
  • RadioLink T16D: Connect via USB-C in USB Joystick (HID) mode; map `SA` (CH5) to Flight Mode and `SB` (CH6) to VTOL Transition.
  • FlySky FS-i6X & Spektrum DX6i: Supported via USB trainer dongle or Spektrum WS2000 wireless USB stick.
